# Code of conduct

PeekBuddy follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and
reviews. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks are not
accepted.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/nobuddyorg/PeekBuddy/security/advisories/new)
or by email to the address on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are
handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and
block participants who do not follow this code.
